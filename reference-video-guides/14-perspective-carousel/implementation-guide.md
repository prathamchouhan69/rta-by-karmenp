# Video 14 — 3D perspective carousel

## My question and answer

**Question:** How should the final reference's perspective carousel be used?

**My direction:** Adapt the 3D carousel for browsing selectable items, with
KARM styling.

## Implementation guide

- Use this for an existing selectable-item browsing flow where a carousel
  improves discovery; do not add it to unrelated screens.
- Keep the active item, selection action, and navigation controls clear.
- Use restrained perspective and KARM colors, preserving the incumbent visual
  identity.
- Support touch and keyboard and provide a reduced-motion/list alternative.
- Do not invent a catalogue or new item data just to use the effect.
