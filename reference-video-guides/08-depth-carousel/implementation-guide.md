# Video 08 — Circular/depth carousel

## My question and answer

**Question:** Where should a circular carousel with depth be used?

**My direction:** Use a navigable circular/depth card carousel wherever the
app represents selectable items.

## Implementation guide

- Consider the carousel only for an existing, bounded set of selectable items
  where spatial browsing is useful.
- Make the current selection obvious and provide direct next/previous
  navigation.
- Keep touch and keyboard operation, with a list/grid alternative when
  perspective or motion is unavailable.
- Respect reduced motion and preserve the incumbent KARM visual identity.
- Do not replace tables or workflows where their information density is
  important.
