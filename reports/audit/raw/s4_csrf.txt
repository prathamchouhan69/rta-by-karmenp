=== S4: Forged Cross-Site POST Without CSRF Token Blocked ===

Proxy requires Idempotency-Key header for all writes (POST/PUT).
Test: POST to /api/v1/invoices without Idempotency-Key:
{"ok":false,"error":{"code":"IDEMPOTENCY_REQUIRED","message":"Idempotency-Key is required for writes"}}

The Idempotency-Key acts as CSRF protection (must be generated by client, not automatically sent by browser).
No separate CSRF token cookie/header.

PASS - Writes blocked without Idempotency-Key (serves as CSRF protection)
